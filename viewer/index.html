About this map

Set a river level at the gauge and a bay level, and the map shows land below that water surface that connects to the river. It is a screening tool, not a flood study: it ignores rainfall on the floodplain, stormwater, levees, bridges and how water moves over time. For real flood risk use your council's flood maps (for example Brisbane's Flood Awareness Maps) and Bureau of Meteorology warnings.
